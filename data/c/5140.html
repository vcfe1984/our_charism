Let us give each other our hand and walk together toward the Heavenly Homeland1

Directory of the Third Order, 119

My dear members of the Third Order and friends:

Welcome to you all!

Blessed be God who grants us this joyful meeting with you, the lay people and friends of the Institute of the Incarnate Word, who have come from more than 30 different countries! Immense is the joy of being able to welcome you and to express to you personally our appreciation and gratitude for your magnificent contribution to the cause of Christ.

Our meeting is made more significant by the fact that we are here in Rome, the seat of the Vicar of Christ on earth, as “a single Family, united by the same faith, the same ends, the same mission, the same charism, the same character and the same spirit”2. This represents an unparalleled occasion to bear witness together to our perfect communion with the Church and to confess that our motto is “with Peter and under Peter”3 and that we have no greater aspiration than that of serving the spread of the Kingdom of Christ4.

You –who have chosen to be the leaven that renews humanity from within– are the answer of God, who is always close to men, to the innumerable needs of evangelization of this world, and an irreplaceable support for the mission of our Institute.

Because what is ours is to bring the Incarnate Word to all men, to permeate the values of society with the fragrance of Christ, to transform the hearts of men with His adorable Presence, to undertake epic works for His cause; it is to make the love of Christ reign in families, it is to kindle the burning lamp of the truth of the Incarnate Word in the four corners of this world, even in the most difficult situations and in the most adverse conditions. And it is you, the courageous and consistent witnesses of the duty and mission of the evangelization of cultures, who help us to bring Christ to the most distant places; to bring reality under the lordship of the Lord, inculturating the Gospel in the most hidden recesses of human life.

The collaboration of all of you, tertiaries and friends, in all our missions represents a most important and effective help so that the mission entrusted to us within the Church may expand and reach those most hidden places and spheres of society (proper to lay life) which it would be very difficult for us to reach otherwise. I assure you that your help in our missions is vital.

Without a doubt these are days of special blessings for everyone. It is my fervent prayer for each one that this first international meeting of the members of the Third Order and friends of the IVE may increase the family spirit among us and serve as an incentive so that, on returning to your countries, you may continue working with great dedication to “order temporal affairs according to God5”6, knowing that you are not alone in the mission but have a whole Religious Family that supports you and expects great things of you. Because believe me, on committed lay people like you “depends in great part the extension of the Kingdom of God and the mission of our Religious Family in the world”7. For the close union of forces is the only thing that avails to achieve fully all the ends of the apostolate of our Institute and to protect its goods effectively8.

As Don Orione said to his own: “let us give each other our hand and walk together toward the Heavenly Homeland. Let us edify one another with mutual good example”9. I encourage you with all my heart to continue bearing witness to Christ, the Incarnate Word, always and everywhere.

I could not conclude without first expressing my deep and heartfelt gratitude to all those who organized this event: priests, religious, Servant Sisters, benefactors, and of course, once again: our dear lay people and friends.

I thank all of you for the great effort you have made to come. I ask you kindly, on returning to your homes, to bring my words of encouragement and very great appreciation to all the members of the Third Order who were unable to come, and my most sincere thanks for their support of our missions.

Invoking upon each one the protection of Mary, Queen of Apostles and Star of the new evangelization, I wish you every good in time and in eternity.

In Christ, the Incarnate Word,

Superior General
