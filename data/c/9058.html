Formula of consecration of an IVE Parish to the Blessed Virgin of Luján

O Blessed Virgin of Luján, powerful mediatrix between God and men, through whose hands come to us from Heaven all the favors and benefits we obtain, moved by your maternal tenderness and your ever-immense compassion, today we gather before your throne to express our love to you and to honor you as good children do their mothers.

Most Holy Virgin of Luján!

Evangelizer of all peoples, and Queen of the Institute of the Incarnate Word, to which you were pleased to entrust the mission in this parish.

On this solemn day, inspired by the most sweet gentleness with which you bend down toward us, we, your faithful of the Parish [name of the parish], draw near to You, and before the whole heavenly court we wish to consecrate to you in perpetuity our entire Parish, with all its families, with all our children and young people, with all its married couples, with all its elderly and sick, with all its priests and religious sisters, with all the religious and priestly vocations that, through your intercession and for You, we would like to see flourish among us, with all its poor and its professionals, and all its good enterprises and institutions, present and future.

Mother of the Incarnate Word, Immaculate Virgin Mary, You who one day willed to remain immovably among us: Look upon us, beloved Mother; here are your children.

Through You, most sweet Lady, with You, in full union with your intentions and for You, we, your children of the Parish [name of the parish], wish from this day forward to live to honor and glorify you, and so fulfill the most sweet duty of loving you in time and for all eternity.

O Virgin of Luján, she of the sky-blue and white mantle, keep in your suppliant hands all the souls here present, those who are no longer with us and those who will come after us. We beg you, Virgin Queen of our Parish and of our lives: convert sinners, dry the tears of those who suffer, relieve the poor, help families wounded by violence, lies and selfishness; guard the flower of purity of these your children and young people; increase the love of spouses, so that they may always walk in the light of your Son; strengthen in faith the catechists and the laity of our parish, so that they may be ever more committed and available for the service of the Gospel; raise up among us abundant vocations to the priesthood and to consecrated life, who will hold as their most sublime office the guiding of many other souls to the love of your Divine Son; may all the priests who pass through here always be “true servants of Christ and stewards of the mysteries of God.” And finally, beloved Mother, grant that all of us together, under the protection of your precious help and example, may work united and without rest to plant the Gospel of the beatitudes, and be authentic apostles of the evangelization of culture.

Here we are, Blessed Virgin of Luján,

from today and forever all the faithful of the Parish [name of the parish] are your happy servants. To You we belong irrevocably.

Missionary of Heaven, bless us and bless all our works of apostolate which, for the glory of the Incarnate Word and through your intercession, we trust we may be able to carry out.

† A. M. D. G.
