Consecration of the Parish of St. Louis King of France (Ecuador)

O Most Holy Virgin of Guadalupe, Mother of the True God through whom one lives1,

moved by your maternal tenderness and the incomparable beauty of your face, today we gather before your throne to express our love for you and to honor you as good children do with their mothers.

Holy Virgin of Guadalupe!

Most beautiful Lady,

Heavenly Empress of Christian America2.

On this solemn day on which we celebrate your Immaculate Conception, inspired by the most sweet gentleness with which you bend down toward us, we, these your faithful of the Parish of St. Louis King of France, draw near to You, and in the presence of the Archbishop and of our Superiors and before the whole heavenly court we wish to consecrate to you in perpetuity our entire Parish, with all its families, with all our children and young people, with all its married couples, with all its elderly and sick, with all its priests and women religious, with all the religious and priestly vocations that through your intercession and for You we would like to see flourish among us, with all its poor and its professionals, and all its good enterprises and institutions, present and future.

Mother of God, Immaculate Virgin Mary, You who one day said to St. Juan Diego: ‘Am I not here, I who am your Mother? Are you not under my shadow? Are you not, perchance, in my lap?’3 and who delight in being the Compassionate Mother of those who honor you by trusting in your intercession4: Look upon us, Beloved Mother; behold your children. Be our Queen, Most Pure Lady. Receive us under your mantle as your humblest servants.

Through You, Most Sweet Lady, with you, in full union with your intentions and for You, we, these your children of the Parish of St. Louis King of France, wish from this day forward to live to honor and glorify you, and so fulfill the most sweet duty of loving you in time and for all eternity.

O Virgin of Guadalupe, you of the eyes of infinite tenderness, keep within your suppliant hands all the souls here present, those who are no longer with us and those who will come after us. We beseech you, Virgin Queen of our Parish and of our lives: convert sinners, dry the tears of those who suffer, relieve the poor, help families wounded by violence, lies and selfishness; guard the flower of purity of these your children and young people; increase the love of spouses, so that they may always walk in the light of your Son; strengthen in faith the catechists and the laity of our parish so that they may be ever more committed and available for the service of the Gospel; raise up among us abundant vocations to the priesthood and to consecrated life, who will hold as their most sublime office the guiding of many other souls to the love of your Divine Son; may all the priests who pass through here always be “true servants of Christ and stewards of the mysteries of God”5. And finally, Beloved Mother, grant that all of us together, under the protection of your precious help and example, may work united and without rest to implant the Gospel of the beatitudes, and that we may be authentic apostles of the evangelization of culture.

Here we are, Most Holy Virgin of Guadalupe;

from today and forever all the faithful of the Parish of St. Louis King of France are your blessed servants. To You we belong irrevocably.

Heavenly Missionary of the New World, bless us and bless all our works of apostolate and all the projects that, for the glory of the Incarnate Word and through your intercession, we trust we may be able to carry out.

† A. M. D. G.

Guayaquil, Ecuador, December 8, 2018
