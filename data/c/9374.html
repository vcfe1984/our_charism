(Santiago del Estero 1730 + Buenos Aires 1799. Argentina)

Memorial

From the Common of Saints: for a holy woman

Entrance Antiphon

Lord, the just one rejoices in your strength,
and how greatly he delights in your victory.
You have fulfilled the desires of his heart.

Collect
Almighty and merciful God,
who in Saint María Antonia de San José,
tireless pilgrim of the spiritual exercises,
gave your Church a model of self-giving in proclaiming the Gospel;
grant us, through her intercession, to meditate constantly on the mystery of your Son,
so as to go out to meet the poor and the forsaken.
Through our Lord Jesus Christ, your Son,
who lives and reigns with you in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God,
for ever and ever.

Prayer over the Offerings
O God, we humbly ask that the sacrifice we offer
in memory of Saint María Antonia de San José
may be effective for our salvation and pleasing to your mercy.
Through Jesus Christ, our Lord.

Communion Antiphon Cf. Mt 16:24

The Lord says: Whoever wishes to follow me
must deny himself, take up his cross and follow me.

Prayer after Communion
Merciful God, we implore you
that the divine power of this sacrament,
celebrated in memory of Saint María Antonia de San José,
may always enlighten us, so that we may experience the desire for holiness
and respond faithfully with good works.
Through Jesus Christ, our Lord.

Readings

Those whom he justified, he also glorified

A reading from the Letter of the Apostle Saint Paul to the Christians of Rome 8:26-30

Brothers and sisters:
The Spirit comes to the aid of our weakness, for we do not know how to pray as we ought; but the Spirit intercedes with inexpressible groanings. And he who searches hearts knows what the desire of the Spirit is, and knows that his intercession on behalf of the saints is in accord with the divine will. We know, moreover, that God arranges all things for the good of those who love him, of those whom he called according to his design. Indeed, those whom God knew beforehand he predestined to reproduce the image of his Son, so that he might be the Firstborn among many brothers; and those he predestined he also called; and those he called he also justified; and those he justified he also glorified.

The Word of God

Or

Put the gifts you have received at the service of others

A reading from the first Letter of the Apostle Saint Peter 4:7b-11

Dear brothers and sisters:
Have the moderation and sobriety needed in order to pray. Above all, love one another intensely, because “love covers all sins.” Practice hospitality without complaining. Put the gifts you have received at the service of others, as good stewards of the manifold grace of God. Whoever has received the gift of the Word, let him teach it as the Word of God. Whoever exercises a ministry, let him do so as one who receives that power from God, so that God may be glorified in all things through Jesus Christ. To him be glory and power for ever and ever! Amen.

Responsorial Psalm 15:1-2a. 5. 7-8. 11

R. You are, Lord, my only good!
Protect me, my God, for I take refuge in you.
I say to the Lord: “Lord, you are my good.”
The Lord is the portion of my inheritance and my cup;
you decide my lot! R.

I will bless the Lord who counsels me;
even at night my conscience instructs me!
I keep the Lord always before me
:he is at my side, I shall never waver. R.

You will make known to me the path of life,
filling me with joy in your presence,
with eternal happiness at your right hand.
You are the portion of my inheritance, Lord! R.

Alleluia
“Remain in my love;
whoever remains in me, and I in him, bears much fruit,”
says the Lord.

Gospel

I no longer call you servants; I call you friends if you do what I command you

* The Gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ according to Saint John 15:9-17

At the Hour of passing from this world to the Father, Jesus said to his disciples:
As the Father has loved me,
so I too have loved you.
Remain in my love.
If you keep my commandments, you will remain in my love,
just as I have kept my Father's commandments and remain in his love.
I have told you this so that my joy may be yours,
and that this joy may be complete.
This is my commandment:
love one another
as I have loved you.
There is no greater love
than to give one's life for one's friends.
You are my friends if you do what I command you.
I no longer call you servants,
because the servant does not know what his master is doing;
I call you friends, because I have made known to you everything I heard from my Father.
It was not you who chose me, but I who chose you,
and I appointed you to go and bear fruit,
and that this fruit may last.
So whatever you ask the Father in my Name,
he will grant it to you.
What I command you is to love one another.

Martha welcomed him into her house. Mary has chosen the better part

* A reading from the holy Gospel according to Saint Luke 10:38-42

As they were walking along, Jesus entered a village, and a woman named Martha welcomed him into her house. She had a sister called Mary, who, seated at the Lord's feet, was listening to his Word. Martha, who was very busy with the household chores, said to Jesus: “Lord, does it not matter to you that my sister leaves me alone with all the work? Tell her to help me.” But the Lord answered her: “Martha, Martha, you are anxious and troubled about many things, and yet few things, or rather, only one thing is necessary. Mary has chosen the better part, which will not be taken from her.”
