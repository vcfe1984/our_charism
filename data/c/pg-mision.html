Purpose of this app

Our Charism1 is dedicated to spreading the charism and the abundant spiritual patrimony of the Institute of the Incarnate Word. Here its religious will find material on the various elements that express the identity and configuration of consecrated life in the Institute according to its “particular style of sanctification and apostolate”2. Although the page is intended mainly for the religious of the Religious Family of the Incarnate Word, with the aim of increasing love for their own Institute and of reaffirming and cultivating its proper identity in the Church, members of the Third Order and the general public will also be able to draw nourishment from the patrimony of the Institute.

Convinced that “the growing configuration of the religious to Christ is brought about in conformity with the charism and norms of the Institute to which he belongs”3, we trust that the unfathomable spiritual wealth that God has granted to the Institute of the Incarnate Word, some samples of which may be found here, constitutes a precious legacy for future generations of religious, so that, by continually returning, with the necessary adaptations, to the spirit bequeathed by the Founder and to the fulfillment of the Proper Law, they may conform themselves ever more and ever better to the ideal of the religious of the Incarnate Word.

“To enter a religious family also means to make its charism and spirituality one’s own, to accept it as the framework within which the life offered to the Lord must unfold through the gift of self”4.

−St. John Paul II
