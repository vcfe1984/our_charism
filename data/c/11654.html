September 23

The Saint

Saint Pio of Pietrelcina, Francesco Forgione, priest of the Order of Friars Minor Capuchin, devoted himself in the convent of San Giovanni Rotondo in Apulia to the spiritual direction of the faithful and the reconciliation of penitents. He showed particular concern for the poor and needy. On this day in 1968 he ended his earthly pilgrimage, conformed to Christ crucified.

The Institute's Proper Law

Directory of Spirituality

“We should learn how to discover ourselves and others in the Heart and the wounds of Christ. Then we will understand what we pray for when we say, ‘Within your wounds, hide us,’ since ‘we are made Christ.’”1

The Founder

“From Sezze we made a pilgrimage to the tomb of Blessed Padre Pio of Pietrelcina. Incredible, his life and work! […] He is considered the first stigmatized priest, and in this century of rationalism he gave a particular witness to the supernatural. […] I prayed for everyone, that always and everywhere we might be witnesses to the supernatural. The principal aspect I wish to highlight in several themes is Padre Pio's mission as a witness to the supernatural world. That is how we shall consider him in the celebration of Mass—‘Mass is a tremendous mystery,’ he said—in the meaning of his stigmata and his charismatic gifts, and in his contact with spiritual beings of the other world. I add that Padre Pio's canonization was an occasion to entrust to his protection our entire Religious Family of the Incarnate Word, which venerates him with great devotion.”2

Excerpt from El Señor es mi Pastor. Memoria y Profecía. Working translation.
