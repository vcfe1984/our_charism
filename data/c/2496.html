O most lovable Patriarch St. Joseph! From the abyss of my littleness and misery I contemplate you, with emotion and joy of soul, on your throne in heaven, as the glory and delight of the blessed, but also as father of orphans on earth, consoler of the sorrowful, protector of the helpless, helper of the Angels and Saints before the throne of God, of your Jesus and of your holy Spouse.

Therefore I, poor, helpless, sorrowful and needy, direct to you today and always my tears and sorrows, the pleas and cries of my soul, my repentance and my hopes; and today especially I bring before your altar and your image a sorrow for you to console, an ill for you to remedy, a misfortune for you to prevent, a need for you to relieve, a grace for you to obtain for me and for my loved ones.

And to move you and oblige you to hear me and obtain it for me, I will ask and entreat you for thirty consecutive days, in reverence for the thirty years you lived on earth with Jesus and Mary; and I will ask you urgently and confidently, invoking all the titles you have to take pity on me, and all the reasons I have to hope that you will not delay in hearing my petition and remedying my need; my faith in your goodness and power being so certain that, on feeling it, you will also feel obliged to obtain and give me even more than I ask and desire.

  1. I ask it of you by the divine goodness that moved the Eternal Word to become incarnate and be born in poor human nature, as Son of God, God-man and God of man.
  2. I beseech it of you by your immense anxiety when you felt obliged to leave your holy Spouse.
  3. I beg it of you by your most sorrowful resignation in seeking a stable and a manger as palace and cradle for God born among men.
  4. I implore it of you by the painful and humiliating circumcision of your Jesus, and by the holy, glorious and most sweet Name that you gave Him by command of the Eternal One.
  5. I entreat it of you by your alarm on hearing from the angel of the death decreed against your Divine Son, by your most obedient flight into Egypt, by the hardships and dangers of the journey, by the extreme poverty of the exile, and by your anxieties on returning from Egypt to Nazareth.
  6. I ask it of you by your most sorrowful affliction of three days on losing your Son, and by your most sweet consolation on finding Him in the Temple, and by your ineffable happiness during the thirty years you lived in Nazareth with Jesus and Mary, subject to your authority and providence.
  7. I beg and hope for it by the heroic sacrifice with which you offered the victim, your Jesus, to the Eternal God for the Cross and for death for our sins and our Redemption.
  8. I entreat it of you by the sorrowful foreknowledge that made you contemplate every day those infant hands later pierced on the cross by sharp nails; that head, which rested so sweetly on your breast, crowned with thorns; that divine body, which you pressed to your heart, naked, bloodied and stretched out upon the arms of the cross; that last moment in which you saw Him breathe His last and die.
  9. I ask it of you by your most sweet passing from this life in the arms of Jesus and Mary, and your entrance into the limbo of the just and at last into heaven.
  10. I beseech it of you by your joy and your glory when you beheld the Resurrection of your Jesus, His ascent and entrance into the heavens, and His throne as immortal King of the ages.
  11. I entreat it of you by your ineffable bliss when you saw your most holy Spouse come forth risen from the tomb and be taken up into heaven by the Angels and crowned by the Eternal One and enthroned on a seat beside your own.
  12. I ask it of you, and beg, and confidently hope for it, by your labors, hardships and sacrifices on earth, and by your triumphs and glories and happy beatitude in Heaven with your Son Jesus and your spouse Holy Mary.

O my good St. Joseph! I, inspired by the teachings of Holy Church and of her doctors and theologians, and by the universal sense of the Christian people, feel within me a mysterious strength that encourages and compels me to ask you, to beseech you, and to hope that you will obtain for me from God the great and extraordinary grace that I am about to place before your image and before your throne of goodness and power in heaven.

(Here, lifting up the heart on high, ask the Saint with loving insistence for the grace desired).

Obtain also for my own, and for those who have asked me to pray for them, all that they desire and that is good for them.

St. Joseph, pray for us, that we may be made worthy of the promises of Our Lord Jesus Christ.

Prayer: O God, who in your ineffable providence deigned to choose blessed Joseph as Spouse of your most holy Mother, grant that, as we venerate him as our protector on earth, we may deserve to have him as our intercessor in heaven. O God, who live and reign for ever and ever. Amen.
