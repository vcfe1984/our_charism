SAINT JOSÉ GABRIEL DEL ROSARIO BROCHERO

Priest

The Cura Brochero was born on March 16, 1840, near Villa Santa Rosa (Province of Córdoba). At 16 he entered the Major Seminary of Córdoba, “Our Lady of Loreto,” where he received his priestly formation, and he pursued his philosophical and theological studies at the University of Córdoba. He was ordained a priest on November 4, 1866. A typical feature of his priestly life was his presentation of the Gospel in vivid language, close to the understanding of simple people. His concern was to enlighten the life of his faithful from the Word of God, not in a general and abstract way but applied to the concrete circumstances of life. He was appointed Pastor of Villa del Tránsito (today Villa Cura Brochero), from where he carried out his intense pastoral ministry. He died there, sick with leprosy and blind, on January 26, 1914, at the age of 74.

Collect

Lord, from whom every perfect gift comes,
you made Saint José Gabriel del Rosario, priest, illustrious
for his missionary zeal, his evangelical preaching
and his poor and self-giving life;
grant that, following his example and with his intercession,
pastors and the people entrusted to them may always be your faithful
missionary disciples of the Gospel.
Through our Lord Jesus Christ, your Son, who, being God,
lives and reigns with you in the unity of the Holy Spirit,
all honor and all glory for ever and ever.

Prayer over the Offerings

Almighty God,
we humbly implore you that,
just as these gifts presented
in honor of Saint José Gabriel del Rosario
manifest the glory of your divine power,
so too they may make the effects
of your salvation grow in us.
Through Jesus Christ, our Lord.

PREFACE OF HOLY PASTORS

The presence of holy Pastors in the Church

V. The Lord be with you
R. And with your spirit
V. Lift up your hearts
R. We have lifted them up to the Lord
V. Let us give thanks to the Lord our God
R. It is right and just

It is truly right and just,
our duty and our salvation,
always and everywhere to give you thanks,
Lord, holy Father, almighty and eternal God,
through Christ our Lord.

For you grant us the joy of celebrating today
the feast of Saint José Gabriel del Rosario,
strengthening your Church by the example of his life,
the teaching of his doctrine
and the help of his intercession.
Therefore, united with the angels and saints,
we praise you, full of joy

Communion Antiphon

This is the faithful and prudent steward, whom the Lord has set
over his household to give out the ration of wheat at the proper
time (P.T. Alleluia)

Prayer after Communion

Nourished by your sacred gifts, we ask you,
almighty God, that, following the example
of Saint José Gabriel del Rosario,
we may serve you with renewed fervor and,
through tireless charity,
manifest your charity to all our brothers and sisters.
Through Jesus Christ our Lord
