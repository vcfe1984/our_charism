Mass of St. John Bosco

Priest

Entrance Antiphon 1 Kgs 5:9

The Lord gave him wisdom and understanding
and a heart as vast as the shores beside the sea

Collect

Lord our God,
in your providence you gave us St. John Bosco,
father and teacher of the young,
who, under the guidance of the Virgin Mary,
labored with tireless dedication for the good of the Church;
stir up in us too the same apostolic charity,
which may impel us to seek the salvation of our brothers
in order to serve you, the one and supreme good.
Through our Lord Jesus Christ.

Prayer of the Faithful

In this celebration in honor of the father and teacher of youth, let us pray, brothers and sisters, to God the Father, who is always ready to welcome the prayers of his children.

R. Hear our prayer, O Father.

1. For the Pope, the bishops, priests and deacons: that they may proclaim the Gospel of
truth and joy, giving joyful witness to their vocation, let us pray to the Lord.

2. For the Salesians and the Daughters of Mary Help of Christians: that, imitating St. John
Bosco in the total gift of his life to the service of the young, they may know how to form in them the true image of Christ, let us pray to the Lord.

3. For young people who are abandoned, marginalized and without ideals: that in every son of
St. John Bosco they may find a friend who helps them to direct their lives according to the Gospel,
let us pray to the Lord.

4. For all who work in the Christian education of the young: that they may see in their
holy patron a teacher and guide from whom to draw inspiration for their mission, let us pray to the Lord.

5. For all of us: that we may know how to practice daily the Gospel teaching of
mutual welcome, so that one day we may be received by Jesus Christ, let us pray to the Lord.

O God, ever faithful, who gave St. John Bosco a great and generous heart, hear our prayer, so that, guided by your Spirit, we may know how to live each day what we ask of you in faith. Through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Or:

Brothers and sisters, as we celebrate the solemnity of St. John Bosco, faithful servant of the Church and educator of the young, let us direct our petitions with faith to God, rich in mercy and goodness.

R. Guide us, Father, along the way of life.

1. For the holy Church of God: that the Lord, who raises up saints according to the
times and needs, may raise up in our age too zealous apostles, teachers of the faith and spiritual guides, let us pray to the Lord.

2. For ecclesial communities: that, overcoming every form of selfishness,
they may work with untiring zeal for the poor, the weak and the least, and strive
to overcome all marginalization, let us pray to the Lord.

3. For young people: that they may know how to grasp the true demands of history and
work generously for a more human and Christian tomorrow, let us pray to the Lord.

4. For parents and educators: that they may help families, parishes and
schools to develop a sense of shared responsibility in the formation of
adolescents and young people, let us pray to the Lord.

5. For the Salesian family: that, attentive to the signs of the times and imitating the
example of St. John Bosco, it may work boldly for the complete salvation of man, let us pray to the Lord.

6. For those of us gathered here: that we may always seek what is
truly true, just and virtuous, and make our life an unceasing thanksgiving to God, let us pray to the Lord.

Lord and our Father, give us a faith rich in works and a charity without borders, so that in the situations of each day we may be, like St. John Bosco, signs and bearers of your love for the young. Through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Prayer over the Offerings

Accept, holy Father,
the sacrifice we offer you with sincere faith,
and grant that we may love you above all things, so that our
life may be a hymn in your honor. Through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Preface

V. The Lord be with you.
R. And with your spirit.

V. Lift up your hearts.
R. We lift them up to the Lord.

V. Let us give thanks to the Lord our God.
R. It is right and just.

It is truly right to give you thanks,
and our duty to glorify you,
great and merciful God,
through Christ, your Son and our Savior.

For you, Father of eternal love,
raised up in the Church St. John Bosco
as friend, brother
and father of the young,
to lead them
along the way of salvation.

He, with a prophetic gaze
toward the new times,
prepared them to face life
with upright wisdom
and a faith rich in works.

And, moved by your Spirit,
he gave rise to a great family
to continue throughout the earth
his mission as teacher and father.

Therefore we,
gathered in festive assembly,
sing to you a hymn
of adoration and praise,
and united with the angels and the saints
we proclaim without ceasing
the hymn of your glory.
Holy, Holy, Holy…

Communion Antiphon Mt 18:5

Whoever welcomes a child such as this in my name
welcomes me —says the Lord—.

Prayer after Communion

Father, you have nourished us
with this sacrament of salvation;
grant that we may imitate the tireless activity of St. John Bosco
in leading the young to the love of Christ, your Son,
inexhaustible source of new life.
Who lives and reigns for ever and ever.

SOLEMN BLESSING

May the Lord, who inspires every good resolve,
protect you always with his grace
and help you to be faithful to the promises of your baptism.
Amen.

May Christ, the good shepherd,
help you to serve your brothers and sisters with joy,
imitating the example of our [your] holy founder.
Amen.

To you who joyfully celebrate
the feast of St. John Bosco,
may the Lord grant that you always walk
in faith, in hope and in love.
Amen.

And may the blessing of almighty God,
the Father, the Son +and the Holy Spirit,
come down upon you.
Amen.
