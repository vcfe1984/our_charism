July 9

Mass of St. Augustine Zhao Rong
priest, and companions, martyrs

Saints Augustine Zhao Rong, priest, Peter Sans i Jordá, bishop, and companions, martyrs, who in various places of China and at various times bore witness to the Gospel of Christ by their words and their deeds, and, for having preached and confessed the faith, suffered persecution, thereby meriting to pass into the eternal banquet of glory (17th/18th centuries).

Entrance Antiphon

The saints who followed in the footsteps of Christ live joyfully in heaven. They shed their blood for love of him, and so they rejoice with Christ forever.

Collect

Lord God,
who in your wondrous providence strengthened your Church
with the witness of the holy martyrs Augustine and his companions,
we ask that your people,
faithful to the mission you entrusted to them,
may live in freedom
and bear witness to the truth before the world.
Through our Lord Jesus Christ, your Son,
who lives and reigns with you in the unity of the Holy Spirit,
God, for ever and ever.

Prayer over the Offerings

Receive, holy Father,
the offerings we present to you
on the feast of your holy martyrs Augustine and his companions,
and grant us the grace
to remain ever steadfast
in confessing your name.
Through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Communion Antiphon Lk 22:28-30

You are the ones who have persevered with me in my trials, and I hand on the kingdom to you, says the Lord; you will eat and drink at my table in my kingdom.

Prayer after Communion

Lord our God,
who shed light on the mystery of the cross
in the glorious death of your martyrs,
hear our plea
and grant that, strengthened by this sacrifice,
we may be faithfully united to Christ
and labor in the Church
for the salvation of all.
Through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Liturgy of the Word

FIRST READING

Jn 5:1-5

What has won the victory over the world is our faith

A reading from the first letter of the apostle Saint John
Dear brothers and sisters:
Everyone who believes that Jesus is the Christ has been born of God; and everyone who loves God who gives being also loves the one born of him. By this we know that we love the children of God: if we love God and keep his commandments. For this is what love for God consists in: that we keep his commandments. And his commandments are not burdensome, for whatever is born of God overcomes the world. And what has won the victory over the world is our faith.
Who is it that overcomes the world, if not the one who believes that Jesus is the Son of God?

The word of God.

RESPONSORIAL PSALM

Psalm 125:1-2ab. 2cd-3. 4-5. 6 (R.: 5)

R. Those who sowed in tears reap with songs of joy.

Or:

R. Alleluia.

When the Lord changed the fortunes of Zion,
it seemed to us we were dreaming:
our mouths were filled with laughter,
our tongues with songs. R.

Even the nations were saying:
The Lord has done great things for them.
The Lord has done great things for us,
and we are glad. R.

May the Lord change our fortunes
like the torrents of the Negeb.
Those who sowed in tears
reap with songs of joy. R.

Going out, he went weeping,
carrying the seed;
coming back, he comes back singing,
bringing in his sheaves. R.

ALLELUIA

Jas 1:12

Blessed the man who endures the trial,
for, once he has been tested,
he will receive the crown of life.

GOSPEL

Jn 12:24-26

If the grain of wheat dies, it bears much fruit

A reading from the holy Gospel according to Saint John
At that time, Jesus said to his disciples:
“I assure you that if the grain of wheat does not fall to the ground and die, it remains barren; but if it dies, it bears much fruit. Whoever loves himself is lost, and whoever hates himself in this world will keep himself for eternal life. Whoever wishes to serve me, let him follow me, and where I am, there also will my servant be; whoever serves me, the Father will reward.”

The Gospel of the Lord.
