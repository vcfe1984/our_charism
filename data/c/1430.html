Beloved Mother!

Prayer in honor of the Queen and Patroness of our Institute, Our Lady of Luján

Most Holy Virgin Mary of Luján, Daughter of God the Father, Mother

of God the Son and Spouse of God the Holy Spirit,

Heart of the Trinity!

At the beginning of time, God gathered all the waters and

called them: sea; when the fullness of time came He gathered all the

graces and called them: Mary¹, Full of grace! So much so, that one

sigh of yours for a soul has more power than all the prayers

of the other saints together², Suppliant Omnipotence! At that very

moment God created an irreconcilable enmity that will last

forever between You, Immaculate Virgin, and Satan: “… I put

enmity between you and the woman, between your offspring and her

offspring…” (Gen 3:14-15).

You are terrible as an army set in battle array!

You ever abhor evil!

You are the Virgin par excellence, you are the singular Virgin

who introduced perfect virginity without any prior example and who

brought forth, by your virginizing power in those who love and serve you,

a constellation of virgins, men and women, over twenty centuries, with a

force as unbending as a tectonic plate, against every advance

of pansexualism, remaining “in any case mystically

effective”³ even in the midst of devastating eroticism⁴. Enclosed garden!

Sealed fountain! “Miracle of miracles”⁵!

You are the Woman of truest renown in all humanity:

“Blessed are you among women!” (Lk 1:42), “From now on all generations

will call me blessed, for the Mighty One has done great things

for me” (Lk 1:48-49)⁶.

In the fullness of time you united to yourself not only the Son of

God, by giving Him flesh and blood as Head of the Mystical Body,

but also all of us, the members of that Head,

Mother of God! Mother of the Church! Neck that joins the Head

to the members and the members to the Head!

During your life in this world you had much to suffer,

Panoply of 7 swords!

At the foot of the cross, as you stood there, what had taken place

at the Incarnation was publicly manifested, when the Son

entrusted you to all the men and women of the world, in the

person of St. John: “Woman, behold your son” (Jn 19:26), Genome

of all redeemed humanity!

To be your devotee, Mary, is a “Charta libertatis”⁷.

Flame of fire and wind! Assumed: a woman's body is in

Heaven! Queen! Former of the saints of the latter times,

Tsunami of graces! That will be when you are better known than ever⁸.

Amen of amens!⁹.

You know who we are and that we need you. We are your slaves

in a maternal slavery of love, who wish to do everything “through

Mary, with Mary, in Mary and for Mary.”

You who stayed beside the river of Luján, stay today and

always deep within our hearts¹⁰.

Amen.
