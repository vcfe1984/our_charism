Homily on the Solemnity of Our Lady of Luján

Dear Religious Family: This beautiful day in May on which we celebrate Our Lady of Luján, the Queen of the Institute, is an altogether special day, a day of grace. A day that speaks to us of the constant manifestations of tenderness and maternal care, of the special intercession, of the great condescension that this most Kind Mother has shown and continues to show us, and which has brought so much consolation to our missionary hearts.

It is a day on which there shines forth more brightly how the Virgin of the sky-blue mantle, crowned with twelve stars, has been pleased to interweave her history with ours, and keeps us safe between her joined hands, in the warmth of her Immaculate Heart.

In truth, this love story of the Blessed Virgin began to be woven on that first May 8, 1630, when, with that first miracle –the miracle of the cart– the Most Pure Conception chose to remain by the Luján River in Argentina to be the Mother, Queen, Lady and Patroness, first of all, of the Argentine people. Who would have thought that today, almost 400 years later, hundreds of her children would have set out to the four corners of the earth, carrying her image everywhere to make her the magnificent guardian of all our missions! And no longer only children of the Argentine people, but also of many other peoples from among whom the Lord has raised up vocations for our Religious Family! God willing, we will continue going to all nations, as her Son commanded us (cf. Mt 28:19-20), always bringing Her, too, to the whole world.

Truly, all of us, the members and the tertiaries of this Religious Family, of such different origins, nationalities, races and cultures, are, one by one, her children, and in Her we were born. We can truly say of our Mother of Luján what Scripture says of Jerusalem, that she is Mother of all peoples: This is the City the Lord founded upon the holy Mountains! The Lord loves the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob. Glorious things are said of you, City of God: ‘I will count Egypt and Babylon among those who know me; Philistines, Tyrians and Ethiopians, all were born in her.’ Thus will it be said of Zion: ‘This one, and that one too, were born in her, and the Most High himself has founded her.’ When he registers the peoples, the Lord will write: ‘This one was born in her.’ And all will sing as they dance: ‘All my springs of life are in you’1.

This most Sweet Mother has proven to us more than enough how much she loves us, and how many times she has made us experience her preference and special affection for this, our little Religious Family, which is honored to have her as Patroness.

To highlight how significant and providential this May 8 is, and to stir up our trust in Her even more, and as an encouragement to our hope, I wish to mention some anniversaries that fall this year and which, like links along one and the same history, make “manifest the dominion and maternal providence that Mary has over all things”2, because they form, as it were, a golden chain that keeps us anchored at the feet of this magnificent Queen, captivated by love.

In the first place, this year marks 385 years since the first chapel was erected to guard and honor the most sweet image of the Virgin of Luján. In that place this Compassionate Mother welcomed “the numerous pilgrims who came there, drawn by the graces that She dispensed to her devotees”3. Certainly that chapel was very rustic and small, but from there the holy image worked unceasing wonders in countless souls. How can we not see in this anniversary a most gentle and providential coincidence, when we think of the shrine that we wish to offer her today in her honor in Tajikistan –a country that has had her as Patroness for 18 years–! A shrine that we trust will be –as that first chapel was– “an oasis in the desert of this life, a beacon of light”4 that enlightens minds with the Truth that saves. A house from which this Exalted Queen and Mother may form and forge true apostles of hers, Marian apostles. Missionaries who, like this “great evangelizer of culture”5, love what is their own while remaining open to universal values, without exaggerated particularisms, without incomprehensible insularity6.

By the merciful design of the same Incarnate Word, also providentially, this year marks 320 years since the death of Black Manuel –a slave native to Africa– who was consecrated to serving the Blessed Virgin for more than 40 years in Luján. He would proudly say: “I belong to the Virgin and no one else,” and he acknowledged “that he had no other master to serve but the Most Holy Virgin.” I think, then, of these three brothers of ours: two from Argentina and one from Egypt (from North Africa), who today are going to consecrate themselves perpetually to God by means of the three religious vows and who, “in order to do so better”7, will consecrate themselves in perpetuity in maternal slavery of love to this most Merciful Mother, so that “She may dispose of everything according to her good pleasure”8. What better feast to give oneself totally to Her as religious of the Institute than that of Mary of Luján, our Mother and Patroness! And what better place than this magnificent basilica of St. Mary Major, the first dedicated to Mary Mother of God in the West!

Moreover, contemplating the example of Black Manuel, who “applied himself with such solicitude to the worship of this Divine Lady, to the point that he never left her Image without light”9, I think of the offering of the men’s monastery in Papua New Guinea and of the women’s monastery in Tajikistan, which today we will joyfully offer to our Mother. So that our monks and contemplative sisters, with their silent holocaust, may be there like that votive lamp that burned unceasingly at the feet of the Virgin of Luján, to thank her forever for the vocations that She herself begets for our Religious Family, and to beg her unceasingly that new vocations may spring up everywhere, ready to embrace the most noble apostolic undertaking that going to the emblematic missions implies, in order “to make Jesus Christ loved and served”10. Great and generous souls who understand that true “worship consists in making one’s own life an offering to God”11, as Black Manuel himself teaches us, and in an eminent way the Blessed Virgin herself with her blessed fiat (Lk 1:38).

Finally, it has been 33 years since that year of 1985 when, “with the approval of the Bishop, Msgr. León Kruk, the first group of our members founded, on February 22 of that year in San Rafael, Argentina, the first house of the Institute which, by common agreement, came to be called “Villa de Luján” and which today houses the Major Seminary “Mary, Mother of the Incarnate Word,” the largest Major Seminary of the Institute12, and which has given to the Church, as we said at the beginning, hundreds of missionaries scattered throughout the world.

We also wish to say, to the honor of this kind Mother, that one year after the offering of the Golden Rose that we made in this very place, our beloved Institute has had a notable increase in vocations, perhaps counting today more vocations than ever before in its history. All of this certainly thanks to the immeasurable intercession and condescension of our Queen and Mother, the Virgin of Luján.

In the face of all this, how can we not make our own the cry of St. Paul, who said to us in the second reading we heard today!: Blessed be God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every kind of spiritual good in the heavens, and has chosen us in him, before the creation of the world, to be holy and blameless in his presence, through love13.

And truly, how many are the gifts with which God has blessed us in Christ! But among the most eminent gifts is certainly the gift of his Most Holy Mother, as the Incarnate Word himself told us in the Gospel we heard a few minutes ago: Here is your mother14.

So, for all that we have just mentioned and for many more things that remain unsaid, our devotion and love for the Virgin of Luján cannot be reduced to just one day or one year, for example, only to this May 8. She is part of our history as a Religious Family! She is an integral part of our spirituality! She is the very depth of the soul of every religious of the Incarnate Word! She is the Mother who gathers us in a tender embrace from very different nations to whisper in our ear: Do whatever He tells you15. She is the ribbon that binds, as in a bouquet, the countless blessings that God sends us.

Through Her the doors of the world are opened to us so that we may go and evangelize. …But let us be aware that love is repaid with love… Let us be Apostles of Mary16, that is, let Her be “the end toward which our acts are directed, the object that captivates our hearts and the motive of the works we undertake”17. And let us be apostles in imitation of Mary, that is: “carrying out the things of God promptly18; serving our neighbor, like Her, even in the simplest tasks”19; founding “all our apostolic enthusiasm” on her Son, the Incarnate God. In such a way that our whole existence may become an offering in thanksgiving and a pledge of the true and perpetual love that we render her.

[Peroratio] Let us ask, then, on this day, the grace for all the members of our Religious Family “that the Blessed Virgin may be the model, the guide, the form of all our acts”20, and that, as our Proper Law says, She may make of us “great saints”21. We ask her this in this Holy Mass.
