October 4

The Saint

Memorial of Saint Francis, who, after a carefree youth, turned to the life of the Gospel at Assisi in Umbria. Finding Christ above all in the poor and needy, he became poor himself and founded the Friars Minor. In his travels he preached the love of God to everyone and even reached the Holy Land. Through his words and actions he showed his desire to follow Christ, choosing to die lying on the bare earth in 1226.

The Institute's Proper Law

Constitutions

67, fourth degree of poverty. “… To accept with joy, for the love of God, privations of necessary things for the sake of holy poverty. It means to boast as Saint Paul through hunger and thirst, through frequent fasting, through cold and exposure (2 Cor 11:27). It was said of Saint Francis of Assisi that ‘no one was as ambitious for gold as was he zealous for poverty.’ This degree constitutes the perfection of poverty.”1

104. “… ‘Brothers, ministers and servants, as well as all the rest, always avoid getting angry or irritated by the sin or the defect of others, for the devil takes advantage of one person's guilt to mislead many. Instead, as best as you can, spiritually assist those who have committed sin, because the healthy do not need a doctor, but the sick do.’”2

154. “… Saint Francis of Assisi said that the simple presence of a religious dressed in the holy habit was a way of preaching.”3

The Founder

“Saint Francis of Assisi (1182–1226), shortly before his death, asks in his Testament that all his brothers always share in his immense veneration for the Eucharist and for priests: ‘And I do this because in this world, I see nothing corporally of the most high Son of God Himself except His most holy Body and Blood, which they receive and they alone administer to others. And I will that these most holy mysteries be honored and revered above all things and that they be placed in precious places.’”4

Excerpt from Mi Parroquia. Cristo Vecino. The introductory words are a working translation; the Testament passage uses Paschal Robinson's published English translation.
