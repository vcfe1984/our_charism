Consecration to the Sacred Heart of Jesus

(Ceremony to be performed by a priest)

V. Our help is in the name of the Lord

R. Who made heaven and earth.

V. The Lord be with you.

R. And with your spirit.

LET US PRAY

Almighty and Eternal God, who approve that the pictures or statues of your saints be painted or sculpted, so that whenever we look upon them we may call to mind their deeds and holiness: we beseech you to deign to bless X and sanctify this image, made in honor and remembrance of the Sacred Heart of your Only-begotten Son, our Lord Jesus Christ. Grant that whoever honors and venerates before it the Sacred Heart of your Only-begotten Son may, through his merits, obtain from you grace now and, afterwards, eternal glory. Through the same Christ our Lord. Amen.

(He sprinkles it with holy water)

GREETING OF WELCOME

Deign, O Lord, in the company of your Mother and our Mother, to visit our Institute and take possession of it. We are convinced that we are not worthy that you should enter here, but we trust in your Mercy, all the more because we feel how great is our need and how sincere our offering.

We need you, Lord, to enlighten us in our doubts, to accompany us in our sorrows, to help us in dangers, to share in our joys, and to bless us now and at the hour of our death. For our part, we would be more than content if, on coming to our Institute, you were to find in it a home like that of Nazareth, a refuge like that of Egypt, or a place of rest like that of Bethany. These, Lord, are our sentiments: to bid you welcome and now to make our consecration to you.

FORMULA OF CONSECRATION

O Most Sacred Heart of Jesus! You made known to St. Margaret Mary your desire to reign over Christian families.

We come today to proclaim your absolute dominion over our Institute. We wish to live, from now on, by your life; we wish that within our Institute the virtues may flourish in our minds through the simplicity of our faith, and in our hearts through love for you alone, striving to keep this flame of love alive with fervent devotion to the Divine Eucharist.

Deign, O Divine Heart, to preside over our relationships, to bless our undertakings, spiritual and temporal, to sanctify our joys and to relieve our sorrows. If ever any one of us has the misfortune to offend you, remind him, O Heart of Jesus, that you are good and merciful toward repentant sinners. And when the hour of separation comes and death comes to bring mourning among us, may all of us, both those who depart and those who remain, be resigned to your loving and eternal designs. We will console ourselves with the thought that a day will come when our whole Institute, gathered together in Heaven, will be able to sing your glories and your benefits for all eternity.

May the Immaculate Heart of Mary and the Glorious Patriarch St. Joseph deign to present this consecration to you and to remind you of it every day of our lives. Amen.

V. Sacred Heart of Jesus

R. Thy kingdom come.

V. Sweet Heart of Mary

R. Be my salvation.

V. Blessed St. Joseph.

R. Pray for us.

A Hail Holy Queen is prayed to the Heart of Mary and an Our Father for the deceased of the family, associating them with the act. The whole place is sprinkled with holy water.
