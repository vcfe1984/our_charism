Article in which Fr. Buela comments on Mt 14:27

I

– When you see the boat of the Church buffeted by contrary winds in the midst of the raging sea…

– When you see the waves, furious and foaming, strike the keel and sweep over the gunwale…

– When the black clouds of evil darken the atmosphere, making the air unbreathable…

– When the roar of the thunder of selfishness deafens men throughout the whole world in violence, wars, destruction and death…

– When the blazing lightning of hatred turns the face of the earth into a Dantesque scene…

– When you see the young grown old before their time because they have let themselves be enslaved by disordered passions…

– When you see adults as if dazed, unable to find their way…

– When television, radio, newspapers and magazines are hotbeds of immorality and corruption…

– When failed marriages and broken families are daily news…

– When newly conceived children are killed with impunity…

– When one is tormented by difficulties, temptations, misunderstandings, persecutions…

– When the epidemic of materialism creeps through the bowels of humanity…

– When, having to stake everything for Christ, you feel dizzy at the thought of what He might ask of you…

I Am!

II

Who is this I?

Second Person of the Most Holy Trinity
announced by the prophets
born in Bethlehem
died on the Cross
rose on the third day…

III

Exodus 3:14

the Resurrection (Jn 11:25)
the living bread come down from heaven (Jn 6:41)
the light of the world (Jn 8:12)

the true life (Jn 15:1)
the alpha and the omega, the First and the Last, the one who is coming (Rev 1:17).
