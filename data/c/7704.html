In my more than 45 years of Masses celebrated in Spanish, for the first time!, I come across a Missal in Spanish of great quality, of such editorial excellence that it adds glory to the heritage of Spain and of its Episcopate.

I will make use of the sheet of recommendations that comes with the text and the indications given on the envelope containing the three CDs (Compact Disks) on music and singing in the Eucharistic liturgy1, which accompany the Missal.

«Recommendations for the care and preservation of this liturgical book»2

Its qualities

The Roman Missal you hold in your hands has been manufactured in keeping with the dignity proper to a liturgical book; from the choice of paper and of the various materials, which are acid- and chlorine-free, to the various production processes:

a) Printing: with environmentally friendly ink.

b)Binding: each process has been carefully chosen to provide maximum durability together with good opening:

– sewn with vegetable thread;

– the spine of the blocks lined with a special reinforcing paper to give the book greater firmness;

– endpapers of ecological card stock to improve the joining of the pages to the cover of the book;

– the ribbon markers are cut with a heat technique to prevent fraying, and are made from two different ribbons, which allows a better grip.

c) Manufacture of the cover: using an ecological material for the cover that imitates leather, made of polyurethane3 and thermo-turning4. The stamping has been pressed deeply into the material, which makes it less subject to wear from handling and friction.

All the materials used in this book hold the certificates of quality and environmental respect, for the sustainable management of forests, required by the current laws of the European Union, as do the printing and binding processes.

Its use

The following practices for the care of the book are recommended so that it may remain in optimal condition for as long as possible:

a) In the way of picking up the book: always try to do so with the book closed, never by a single cover or by a group of pages; hold it with both hands or rest it on a lectern.

b)In the way of opening the book: open it as you normally would and use the tabs (thumb indexes) to turn the pages one by one; do not try to open the book by using the tabs.

c) In the way of storing it: after using it, close the book; when the celebration is over, do not leave it on the lectern or on the altar, but put it in its box. To store it, it is preferable to do so horizontally (not vertically, as is customary) so as not to put too much strain on the binding of the book. It is also important, whenever possible, to keep it in a dark room or in a cupboard.

d)In the way of cleaning it: normally do so with a soft, dry cloth, although, if necessary, dampen the cloth a little and pass it gently over the surface of the cover. Tests have been carried out by putting the cover material in contact with different substances used in liturgical celebrations, such as wine, water, lemon juice and olive oil, and it has been verified that the material is highly resistant to this kind of liquid and to stains in general.

«Sound illustration of the texts set to music in the Roman Missal»

Special mention is due to the place given in the Missal to Sacred Music, reminding us: «The Apostle exhorts the faithful who gather together awaiting the coming of their Lord to sing together psalms, hymns and inspired songs (cf. Col3:16).

Singing is a sign of the heart’s exultation (cf. Acts2:46).

Hence St. Augustine rightly says: «Singing belongs to one who loves»; and from very ancient times comes the famous proverb: «He who sings well prays twice»5.

Music in the new edition of the Roman Missal

In this new edition of the Roman Missal in Spanish a real effort has been made to include an abundant musical appendix, not only the Ordinary, but also other texts, as well as one hundred and three prefaces; all of this to facilitate and encourage the primacy of the solemn liturgy: «Liturgical action takes on a nobler form when the divine offices are celebrated solemnly with song»6.

The musical differences in comparison with other earlier editions of the Missal are not very numerous, but they are significant, above all because of the changes in some texts, which have meant slight alterations to the existing melodies or even the need to create some new ones. This novelty will undoubtedly require an effort to learn it correctly, as well as an important pastoral work to help the faithful become acquainted with the music of the Missal and thus to foster their participation in liturgical actions. For this reason the material we present in triple-CD format has been prepared.

On the musical content

l. The «Amen» of the greeting, of the prayers, of the doxology, etc., which has become popular in our communities is no longer the Gregorian one (sol-sol-la). For this reason it has been proposed that the «Amens» incorporated into the Ordinary be those the people already sing with only two notes: sol-la. Moreover, it is a way of making the criterion uniform, since some current liturgical books include the Gregorian model, others the popular model, and in one and the same liturgical book we may even find both.

2. The musical setting of the three formulas of the Penitential Act has been included.

3. In the Liturgy of the Word three simple melodic models have been included for singing the readings (first, second and Gospel). These models are not intended to replace the traditional «Gregorian tones for the singing of the readings», but to serve as a help for communities that are not proficient in Gregorian chant, so that they may, in a simple way, sing the Liturgy of the Word for greater solemnity.

The criterion has been to use melodic elements in a graduated way through the «mediant cadence» in the first and second readings, and to incorporate in the Gospel the melodic formula for the «highlighted words» of Christ (la-do-ti-sol-ti-la), creating an ornamental progression whose melody evokes the account of the institution of the Eucharist, thus establishing a musical parallel between the summit of the Liturgy of the Word (Gospel) and that of the Eucharistic liturgy (consecration).

The same has been done with the acclamation to the Gospel (The Word of the Lord) and its response (Glory to You, Lord Jesus). A new acclamation «in imitation» is proposed, in such a way that the faithful will be able to answer the deacon or priest who sings the Gospel with ease. For this new acclamation the final formula of the response to the acclamation «This is the Mystery of faith» (do-ti-sol-la) has been taken. Thus the assembly responds by singing with the same melodic formula to the Gospel as to the Mysterium fidei.

4. Eight models have been included for the sung response of the prayer of the faithful.

5. This new edition of the Missal includes the melody for singing Eucharistic Prayers I, II, III and IV from the epiclesis onward.

6. At the consecration the appropriate musical adaptation has been made in view of the change in the following text: «which will be poured out for you and for many».

7. The acclamations and responses of the consecration, which have changed in accordance with the new translation criteria, have been adapted musically. Three formulas remain:

a. In the first there are no changes in the acclamation or in the response; only the order of the first and second option varies: «This is the Mystery of faith», or else: «This is the Sacrament of our faith».

b. The second, which is new, is: «Let us acclaim the Mystery of faith», instead of «Acclaim the Mystery of redemption».

The response remains the same as in earlier editions.

c. The third, also new, changes in its acclamation and in its response: «Let us proclaim the Mystery of faith». «Save us, Savior of the world, who have set us free by your cross and resurrection».

8. Two models are offered for singing the Our Father: the one that has already become popular (A) and another adapted from the Gregorian Pater noster (B).

9. In the concluding rite the musical setting of the simple blessing, the episcopal blessing and two melodic models for the solemn blessings (A and B) have been added. One more popular (A) and another adapted from the Gregorian (B).

10. Finally, the 103 prefaces found in the Missal are included, each individually set to music.

We said years ago that the Roman Missal: «Is truly a veritable monument of Castilian, Spanish and Spanish-American literature, which perhaps the Royal Spanish Academy of the Language and its counterparts in the Spanish-speaking countries ought to take more into account»7, and in this most painstaking edition it reaches its summit.

With the appropriate care and correct handling that liturgical books require, as we have indicated here, you will be able to use this volume for many years. The materials hold all the certificates of quality and environmental respect, as do the printing and binding processes. Its texts have also been studied by a member of the prestigious Royal Academy of the Spanish Language. For all these reasons we foresee a qualitative leap in the ars celebrandi and in the ars participandi of the Holy Sacrifice.

We are before a liturgical jewel, after many years of liturgical vulgarities, frivolities and pettiness. Let us hope that this example of liturgical dignity may be imitated by many in Spanish America and wherever Mass is celebrated in the «language of the angels», as Don Marcelino Menéndez y Pelayo put it!
