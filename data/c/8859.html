Formula of perpetual consecration in the

institute of the incarnate word1

For love

of the Father, first origin and supreme end
of consecrated life;
of Christ, who calls us to his intimacy;
of the Holy Spirit, who disposes the soul
to welcome his inspirations.

I, N.N., freely make to God the oblation of my whole being:
to deepen, with an ever more sincere and intense love,
the gift of the evangelical counsels in their Trinitarian dimension;
to be a concrete trace that the Trinity leaves in history,
so that all men may discover the attraction
and the longing for divine beauty;
so that my life may be a living memorial
of the way of existing and acting
of Jesus, the Word made flesh (cf. Jn 1:14),
before the Father and before men.

Therefore, I commit all my strength
not to shy away from the missionary adventure,
to inculturate the Gospel
in the diversity of all cultures,
to prolong the Incarnation of the Word
“in every man, in the whole man
and in all the manifestations of man,”
assuming all that is authentically human,
to be like another humanity of Christ,
to carry out with greater perfection
the service of God and of men.

For this reason, before God our Lord and all his saints,
before N.N., Provincial Superior
of the Institute of the Incarnate Word,
(or: before N.N., who acts in place
of the Provincial Superior of the Institute of the Incarnate Word)
and in the presence of the members of this Institute
and of the Servants of the Lord and the Virgin of Matará,
I vow to live forever
chaste, for the Kingdom of Heaven,
poor, showing that God is the only true wealth of man,
and obedient, unto death on a cross,
so as to follow more intimately the Incarnate Word
in his chastity, poverty and obedience,
according to the evangelical way
laid out in the Constitutions
of the Institute of the Incarnate Word,
and in order to do this better I make a fourth vow
of consecration to Mary in maternal slavery of love.
I ask the intercession of Our Lady, of the Twelve Apostles
and of the other patron saints,
the prayers of my brothers in the Incarnate Word
and of the sisters of the Institute
of the Servants of the Lord and the Virgin of Matará.
May the love and grace of the Most Holy Trinity
help me to be faithful in the work it has begun.

The one receiving the vows responds:

In the name of the Church
and of the Institute of the Incarnate Word
I receive with great joy into our Religious Family
these brothers with perpetual vows,
who have expressed the explicit desire
for a total conformation
with Christ, the Incarnate Word.
