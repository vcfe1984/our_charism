Mass of St. Patrick

Entrance Antiphon Is 52:7

How lovely upon the mountains are the feet of the messenger who announces peace, who brings good news, who proclaims victory!

Collect

O Lord our God,
who sent the bishop Saint Patrick
to evangelize the peoples of Ireland,
grant us, through his merits and intercession,
that all of us who glory in the name of Christians
may always proclaim your wonders to men.
Through our Lord Jesus Christ, your Son,
who lives and reigns with you in the unity of the Holy Spirit,
God, for ever and ever.

Prayer over the Offerings

Hear our supplications, O Lord,
and free us from all our faults,
so that your grace may purify us
through these sacraments
which we now celebrate.
Through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Communion Antiphon Mk 16:15; Mt 28:20

Go into the whole world and proclaim the Gospel: I am with you all days, says the Lord.

Prayer after Communion

O Lord our God,
may the sacraments we have received
strengthen in us the faith
handed down to us by the preaching of the apostles
and preserved with zeal
by your servant Saint Patrick.
Through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Liturgy of the Word

A reading from the first letter of the apostle Saint Peter 4:7b-11

Dear brothers and sisters:
Be moderate and sober, so that you may be able to pray. Above all, keep your love for one another intense, because love covers a multitude of sins. Offer hospitality to one another without complaining.
Let each one, with the gift he has received, place himself at the service of others, as good stewards of the manifold grace of God.
Whoever speaks, let him speak the word of God. Whoever devotes himself to service, let him do it by virtue of the charge received from God. Thus God will be glorified in all things through Jesus Christ, to whom belong glory and power for ever and ever. Amen.

The Word of God.

PSALM Psalm 95:1-2a. 2b-3. 7-8a. 10 (R.: 3)

R.Tell the wonders of the Lord among all the nations.
Sing to the Lord a new song,
sing to the Lord, all the earth;
sing to the Lord,
bless his name. R.

Proclaim his victory day after day.
Tell his glory among the peoples,
his wonders among all the nations. R.

Families of the peoples, acclaim the Lord,
acclaim the glory and might of the Lord,
acclaim the glory of the Lord's name. R.

Say to the peoples: "The Lord is king,
he has made the world firm, and it shall not be moved;
he governs the peoples with justice." R.

A reading from the letter of the apostle Saint Paul

to the Christians of Ephesus 1:3-6. 11-12

Blessed be God, the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who has blessed us in Christ with every kind of spiritual good in heaven, and has chosen us in him, before the creation of the world, to be holy and blameless in his presence, through love. He predestined us to be his adopted children through Jesus Christ, according to the good pleasure of his will, for the praise of the glory of his grace, which he gave us in his beloved Son. In him we have been made heirs, and destined beforehand—according to the prior plan of the One who accomplishes all things according to his will—to be those who have placed their hope in Christ, for the praise of his glory.

The Word of God

ALLELUIA Mk 1:17

Come with me, says the Lord,
and I will make you fishers of men.

GOSPEL

At your word I will let down the nets

A reading from the holy Gospel according to Saint Luke 5:1-11

At that time, the crowd was pressing around Jesus to hear the word of God, as he was standing on the shore of the lake of Gennesaret. He saw two boats that were beside the shore; the fishermen had gone ashore and were washing their nets.
He got into one of the boats, the one belonging to Simon, and asked him to put out a little from the land. Sitting in the boat, he taught the people.
When he had finished speaking, he said to Simon:
"Put out into the deep, and let down your nets for a catch."
Simon answered:
"Master, we have spent the whole night toiling and have caught nothing; but at your word I will let down the nets."
And when they had set to work, they made such a great haul of fish that the net was bursting. They signaled to their partners in the other boat to come and give them a hand. They came and filled both boats, so that they were almost sinking. When he saw this, Simon Peter threw himself at the feet of Jesus, saying:
"Depart from me, Lord, for I am a sinner."
For amazement had seized him and those who were with him at the haul of fish they had taken; and the same was true of James and John, sons of Zebedee, who were Simon's companions.
Jesus said to Simon:
"Do not be afraid; from now on you will be a fisher of men."
They brought their boats to land and, leaving everything, they followed him.
