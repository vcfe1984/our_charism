Proper texts for the celebration of Mass on

the Solemnity of Our Lady of El Cisne

Entrance Antiphon

Hail, holy Mother, Virgin Mother of the King who rules heaven and earth for ever and ever.

Collect

O God, who willed that your mother, the most glorious Virgin Mary,
be venerated in the beautiful image of Our Lady of El Cisne, graciously grant that we may always know how to imitate in this world
the Christian witness of her whose praises
we hope to merit to sing in heaven.
Through our Lord Jesus Christ, your Son, who lives and reigns with you in the unity of the Holy Spirit, God, for ever and ever. Amen.

Prayer over the Offerings

Receive, O Lord, the offerings of your people together with the oblation of this sacrifice, so that, through the intercession of Holy Mary, the Mother of your Son, no good purpose may go unfulfilled, and none of our petitions be in vain. Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

Communion Antiphon

Blessed is the womb of the Virgin Mary, which bore the Son of the eternal Father.

Prayer after Communion

Having received the heavenly sacraments,
with the joy of having celebrated this commemoration of the Blessed Virgin Mary, we implore your mercy, O Lord:
grant that, in imitation of the Virgin, we may worthily devote ourselves to the service of the ministry of our redemption. Through Jesus Christ our Lord. Amen.

Liturgy of the Word

First Reading

A reading from the book of Isaiah 9: 1-6
The people who walked in darkness have seen a great light; upon those who dwelt in lands of shadow, a light has shone.

You have made your people great and made their joy great. They rejoice in your presence as people rejoice at harvest, as they are glad when dividing the spoils. For you have broken their heavy yoke, the bar that weighed on their shoulders and the scepter of their tyrant, as on the day of Midian.

For a child has been born to us, a son has been given to us; he bears upon his shoulders the sign of dominion, and his name shall be: “Wonderful Counselor,” “Mighty God,”
“Everlasting Father,” “Prince of Peace”; to extend his rule with a peace without limits upon the throne of David and over his kingdom; to establish and strengthen it with justice and right, from now on and forever. The zeal of the Lord will accomplish this.

The word of God.

Responsorial Psalm

R. Blessed be the Lord now and forever

Blessed be the Lord,
let his servants praise him.
Blessed be the Lord,
from now on and forever. R.

From the rising of the sun to its setting
Praised be the name of the Lord.
God is above all the nations;
his glory, above the heavens. R.

Who is like the Lord?
Who is equal to our God,
Who has his dwelling on high,
and yet, for all that,
deigns to lower his gaze
to look upon earth and heaven? R.

He raises the helpless from the dust
and lifts the needy from the dunghill,
to seat him among the great,
the leaders of his people. R.

Second Reading

A reading from the letter of the apostle Saint Paul to the Romans 8:28-30

Brothers and sisters: We know that all things work together for the good of those who love God, of those who have been called by Him according to his saving plan.

Indeed, those whom he foreknows he predestines to reproduce in themselves the image of his own Son, so that He may be the firstborn among many brothers. Those whom he predestines he calls; those whom he calls he justifies, and those whom he justifies he glorifies.

The word of God.

Acclamation before the Gospel

R. Alleluia, alleluia.

Hail, Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with you, blessed are you among women.

R. Alleluia

Gospel

X A reading from the Gospel according to Saint John 2:1-11

At that time, there was a wedding at Cana in Galilee, which the mother of Jesus attended. He and his disciples had been invited. When the wine ran short, Mary said to Jesus: “They have no more wine.” Jesus answered her: “Woman, what can you and I do? My hour has not yet come.” But she said to those who were serving: “Do whatever He tells you.”

There were six stone jars there, of about a hundred liters each, used for the purifications of the Jews. Jesus said to those who were serving: “Fill those jars with water.” And they filled them to the brim. Then he said to them: “Now draw some out and take it to the steward.” They did so, and as soon as the steward tasted the water turned into wine, without knowing where it came from, because only the servants knew, he called the bridegroom and said to him. “Everyone serves the better wine first, and when the guests have drunk plenty, the ordinary wine is served. You, on the other hand, have kept the better wine until now.”

This Jesus did in Cana of Galilee; it was the first of his miraculous signs. Thus he revealed his glory, and his disciples believed in Him.

The Gospel of the Lord.
