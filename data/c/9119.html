Text delivered at the moment of presenting a golden rose at the feet of Our Lady of Luján in Tajikistan on the occasion of an anniversary of the missio sui iuris.

Presentation of the Golden Rose to Our Lady of Luján

[Introductory words]

On this solemn day on which we celebrate yet another anniversary of the creation of the Missio Sui Iuris, before Fr. Pedro López, Superior of the missio, and the parishioners of St. Joseph Parish, we wish to offer the Blessed Virgin this golden rose as a sign of perpetual gratitude for her maternal intercession on account of the immense gift that the creation of the missio has meant for the Institute and for the whole Church in this blessed nation, and to implore her to be pleased to make it grow, making the grace of God fruitful in families and raising up in this small portion of the Church many vocations who, with “missionary zeal”1, will proclaim to the world “the Gospel that saves”2.

[Prayer at the moment of the presentation]

Our Lady of Luján,/

chosen from all eternity to be Mother of the Eternal Word, / and Heavenly Patroness of this nation,/ we –your children– belong to you without reserve, /
and today we wish to leave at your blessed feet/ this golden rose/ as a filial gift and an expression of perpetual gratitude/ for the immeasurable gift of having entrusted to the Institute the care of your flock in these blessed lands. / May this rose be in turn a sign of the trusting and unceasing prayer/ that we place in your hands, invoking your favor,/ so that you may grant to your people in Tajikistan/ numerous priestly and religious vocations/ who will wish to consecrate their lives/ “to prolonging the Incarnation of the Word in every man, / in the whole man/ and in all the manifestations of man”3./

Queen of Tajikistan,/ flower without thorns, / blue lily of the valleys, / may the most pure dew of your grace/ make fruitful in the souls of your Tajik people the Gospel preached by the missionaries of this your little Family of the Incarnate Word; / may your unconditional Fiat to the will of the Father/ always find an echo in their hearts and, / may each day, / all of us and those who will come after us, / always find ourselves united, sheltered beneath your maternal mantle. /

Most pure Mother of Luján, kindly accept this golden rose as a gift of gratitude for the wonders that the Almighty has worked through your mediation in the hearts of so many faithful who venerate you in this your maternal home.
