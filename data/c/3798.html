Coronation of Our Lady

Monastery of Velletri – Segni,

[Exordium] Dear Mother and Sisters: first of all, I would like to thank you very much for the invitation you have extended to me. I am very happy to have been able to come and celebrate Holy Mass with you, here in the Chapel of this Monastery, and even more so on the beautiful occasion of the Coronation of the image of Our Lady, Madonna delle Grazie.

The Coronation of Our Lady is without a doubt an occasion of singular importance for a community. For, in doing so, you proclaim the reign of Mary in particular over your hearts and the hearts of the children of this diocese, who are honored to have her as their Mother. But you also commit yourselves to serving her with filial submission. To act in this way is nothing other than to render homage of adoration to God, who is the Father, Son and Spouse of Mary, because it is He who has constituted her Queen of the whole universe.

That is why this coronation is also what is called “a public oath of fealty.” That is, we show our bond of fidelity and total dependence on so most worthy a Queen, because in calling us one day here to Velletri to live in her house, she made herself our Sovereign, and very particularly yours.

Finally, this Coronation of Our Lady which we will carry out today -by the grace of God- is without a doubt a tribute of gratitude and filial love, justly owed to this most tender Mother and most merciful Patroness of this diocese and of the monastery.

Here, then, are the three points that I would like to touch on briefly in this homily, begging the intercession of the Mother of Divine Grace herself.

1. Homage to God

Let us begin with the first: our homage of adoration to God in venerating his Mother.

It belongs to the supreme Goodness and the unfathomable designs of God to have created a multitude of men and women and destined them to an eternal happiness by virtue of the Incarnate Word and through the mediation of the Mother of God.

Now, in the great procession of humanity She rises up, blessed among all women; she is the great woman par excellence: the Most Holy Virgin Mary.

She alone is a great miracle: “Magnum miraculum”1, said St. John Chrysostom, because She alone surpassed and exceeded in excellence and dignity all the earth and all the heavens. For her dignity as Mother of God is quasi-infinite, according to the expression of the Angelic Doctor.

That is why an author said that the Most Holy Virgin “alone among all creatures rose, on the foundation of her humility, to form in a singular and marvelous way the exterior complement of the Trinity itself, she alone also giving it the greatest accidental glory”2. And he went on to say: “In her, the Father, who eternally had only the Divine Son, came to have in time the Daughter he lacked; the Son, who was eternally begotten by the Father without a Mother, had one in Her; and the Spirit of God, who is charity, eternal love, I mean the Holy Spirit, likewise came to obtain in Her the Divine Spouse who was fitting for him and whom he had loved from all eternity”3.

That is why, dear sisters, the Coronation of Our Lady –if you will allow me the expression– is like the payment of a debt owed by God himself to Her. It is as if Christ, on entering in triumph and sitting at the right hand of the Father, “missed” the presence of his Mother, and that is why God himself raises her to heaven to be exalted by the Eternal King himself and to be worthily crowned.

This is what the Magisterium of the Church teaches us, saying: “The Immaculate Virgin, preserved free from every stain of original sin, when the course of her life on earth was finished, was taken up body and soul into heavenly glory, and was exalted by the Lord as Queen of the universe, so that she might be more fully conformed to her Son, Lord of lords (cf. Rev 19:16) and conqueror of sin and death”4.

Yes, Most Holy Mary triumphed by her virtues and triumphed by the most pure flesh that she freely offered in her chaste womb to her Divine Son. That is why the Incarnate Word himself crowns her, saying to her, and I quote here the Song of Songs: Come from Lebanon, my Bride, come from Lebanon, come, you shall be crowned5.

And it is as if the whole universe ought to second the honors offered by God to the Divine Queen. Because, although adoration is due to God alone, God himself wants all the creatures of heaven and earth, for the honor and worship of God himself, to honor this Most Sweet Mother. And so, when in a moment we crown Our Lady as Queen, we are doing nothing other than rendering the just homage of adoration to the Trinity itself, which displays itself so great and splendid in her who is its Daughter, Mother and Spouse.

So the rite of coronation signifies, beyond the acknowledgment of the glorification of Our Lady, thanksgiving to God because he chose her as the virginal Mother of his Son, because Mary was entirely faithful even to the cross, because Jesus gave her to us as Mother and She received us as children. And in her lap we are sheltered from every storm.

2. Oath of fealty

This coronation that we are about to carry out in a moment –a replica of the Coronation of the Mother of God by the Trinity itself– is also, in our case, an act of surrender and dependence on this Precious Mother, in deference to and respect for her majesty and sovereignty6. How many are the immense favors she grants us! And this is truly our intention, because we want to acknowledge and venerate her as who she is: our Sweet Sovereign.

By making her Queen of the monastery and of the hearts of all those who live here now and will live here in the future, we establish the Kindest of all Mothers as the Abbess who “knows how to have compassion on the misery of her daughters”7 and in her most gentle providence grants them all graces. And She is established as the Mistress who, with loving affection but also “with a sure hand, guides her daughters to perfection”8.

Since She is the Queen, She will be the one who presides over all your activities, your prayers, your moments of eutrapelia, and everything you do. This brings with it the ineffable grace of living in the light of her smile, in the warmth of her heart and sustained by her hand.

What is more, by crowning her we express that we want her to reign not only within these walls, but in each of the hearts that beat here, so that through “fidelity to the one Love, which is manifested and strengthened in the humility of a hidden life, in the acceptance of sufferings in order to complete in one’s own flesh what is lacking in the tribulations of Christ (Col 1:24), in silent sacrifice, in abandonment to the holy will of God, in serene fidelity even in the face of the decline of one’s strength and of one’s own influence”9, there may be born the fervent desire for oblative self-giving out of love for one’s neighbor, and so her reign may spread to the ends of the earth.

And we do so with the utmost confidence and without reserve, because we know that this august Lady “will not allow us to honor her with respect without repaying us a hundredfold”10.

3. Tribute of gratitude and filial love

The coronation expresses our gratitude for Mary’s maternal care, our joy at her exaltation as Queen, our confidence in her protection in dangers. Finally, this coronation is a sign of the tribute of gratitude and filial love owed to the Maddona delle Grazie. For one day She welcomed us into this blessed diocese of Velletri‑Segni, and under her virginal mantle she willed to shelter you in this monastery –which is her house– for a very special mission: to pray for priests.

That is why in this simple and welcoming chapel, like another Nazareth, which serves as a dwelling for the Lord of lords and King of kings11, we want to make her Queen, since we are certain that, like Queen Esther, the Immaculate Virgin, who won the heart of God and in whom the Almighty did great things12, will not fail to grant the petitions that you, her daughters, raise to her unceasingly.

It is in this sense that our Directory of Consecrated Life, quoting St. Anthony Mary Claret, says: “the pleas and prayers of a small number of good souls hold back the wrath of God so that he does not let his arm fall upon a people and even upon a nation”13.

That is why, while I thank you very much for the prayers you offer for us and encourage you to continue doing so with great fervor, I too ask our Mother that this crown which today I am very pleased to place on her precious brow may be a figure of your souls. Because good children are the crown of their parents, and not only the children but also the children’s children.

That is why in this Mass we also pray very especially for you, for your families, and for the fruits of the most worthy mission to which Our Lady herself seems to have called you, so that, thanks to the fidelity of each one of you, an immense multitude of souls may weave the crown of the Most Pure and ever Virgin Maddona delle Grazie.

It is my wish that, just as Our Lady holds the Child Jesus in her lap, She may hold you and bring you closer each day to the Incarnate Word.

May Our Lady bless you.
