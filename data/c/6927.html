St. Louis Mary Grignion de Montfort –
April 28

ENTRANCE ANTIPHON

How lovely upon the mountains are the feet of the messenger who announces peace, who brings good news, who proclaims victory and says: City of Zion, your God now reigns. Alleluia

COLLECT
Lord God, who deigned to lead Saint Louis Mary, priest,
along the way of salvation and of the love of Christ
in the company of Most Holy Mary,
grant us, by his example,
that, meditating on the mysteries of divine love,
we may devote ourselves tirelessly to the building up of your Church.
Through our Lord Jesus Christ, your Son,
who lives and reigns with you in the unity of the Holy Spirit,
God, for ever and ever.

Or:
Almighty and eternal God,
who made Saint Louis Mary, priest,
an outstanding witness and teacher of complete self-giving to Christ, your Son,
through Mary, his Mother,
grant that we, following this spiritual way,
may contribute to the spread of your kingdom in the world.
Through our Lord Jesus Christ, your Son,
who lives and reigns with you in the unity of the Holy Spirit,
God, for ever and ever.

PRAYER OVER THE OFFERINGS

Accept, O Lord,
the offering of our priestly service
on the feast of Saint Louis Mary,
and grant that, freed from the cares and selfishness of this world,
we may fully glorify your holy and glorious name.
Through Jesus Christ our Lord

PREFACE

V. The Lord be with you.
R. And with your spirit

V. Lift up your hearts
R. We lift them up to the Lord

V. Let us give thanks to the Lord our God
R. It is right and just.

It is truly right to give you thanks
and beautiful to sing of your mercy,
holy Father, Lord of heaven and earth,
for you have revealed to the poor the mystery of your Kingdom,
through Christ our Lord.

In the school of divine wisdom
you formed Saint Louis Mary
so that, under the impulse of the Holy Spirit,
he might preach to your people the folly of the Cross:

Consecrated wholly to you through love,
and inspired by a heartfelt love for the Virgin Mary,
He called all to renew in faith
the commitments of the baptismal covenant.

And we (in the fullness of paschal glory)
united with the angels and saints
sing with one voice
The hymn of your glory:

Holy, Holy, Holy…

COMMUNION ANTIPHON

This is eternal life:
that they know you, the only living and true God,
and the one you sent, Jesus Christ your Son,
conceived of the Virgin Mary by the power of the Holy Spirit.

PRAYER AFTER COMMUNION

O God, our Father,
by the mysterious power of these sacraments,
confirm us in the faith,
so that, consecrated wholly to you through love,
we may always bear witness to the wisdom of the Gospel
for which Saint Louis Mary
labored tirelessly until death.
Through Jesus Christ our Lord.

SOLEMN BLESSING

May God our Father,
who has gathered us today to celebrate
the feast of Saint Louis Mary,
confirm you in your commitment
of fidelity to his call.
R. Amen.

May Christ the Lord,
who inaugurated his kingdom
through the faith of the Virgin,
grant you to serve God and your brothers and sisters
with the readiness and generosity
of Saint Louis Mary
R. Amen

May the Holy Spirit,
who kindled missionary ardor in Saint Louis Mary,
make you authentic witnesses of the paschal life
for all whom you meet along the way
R. Amen

And may the blessing of almighty God,
the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit,
come down upon you
and remain with you for ever.
R. Amen

Liturgy of the Word

FIRST READING

Acts 1:6-9.14; 2:1-4.42

From the book of the Acts of the Apostles

The Apostles gathered around him, asking: "Lord, is it now that you are going to restore the sovereignty of Israel?" Jesus answered: "It is not for you to know the times and dates that the Father has fixed by his own authority. When the Holy Spirit comes down upon you, you will receive strength to be my witnesses in Jerusalem, in all Judea, in Samaria and to the ends of the earth." In their presence Jesus was lifted up and a cloud hid him.


All of them devoted themselves to prayer in common, together with some women, among them Mary, the Mother of Jesus, and with his brothers. All the disciples were together on the day of Pentecost. Suddenly a sound from heaven, like a strong wind, resounded through the whole house where they were. They saw tongues appear, like flames, which divided and came to rest upon each one of them. They were all filled with the Holy Spirit and began to speak in foreign tongues, each in the language the Spirit gave him.


The believers devoted themselves to the teaching of the apostles, to the communion, to the breaking of the bread and to the prayers.

The Word of God.

RESPONSORIAL PSALM

Ps 102:1-2.3-4.6-7.8-10.19-20

R. His justice passes on to children's children for those who keep his covenant.

Bless the Lord, O my soul,
and all my being, his holy Name.
Bless the Lord, O my soul,
and do not forget his benefits. R.

He forgives all your faults
and heals all your ills.
He rescues your life from the pit
and fills you with grace and tenderness. R.

The Lord does justice
and defends all the oppressed.
He makes known his ways to Moses
and his mighty deeds to the children of Israel. R.

The Lord is compassionate and merciful,
slow to anger and rich in clemency;
he is not always accusing
nor does he hold a grudge forever. R.

He does not treat us as our sins deserve
nor repay us according to our faults.
The Lord has set his throne in heaven;
His sovereignty rules the universe. R.

SECOND READING

1 Cor 1:17-25

From the first letter of Saint Paul to the Corinthians

Brothers and sisters, in fact the Messiah did not send me to baptize, but to announce the good news, without any eloquence, so that the cross of the Messiah may not be emptied of its power.


For the message of the cross is folly to those who are perishing; to those who are being saved it is the power of God. As it is written: "I will do away with the wisdom of the wise and confound the understanding of the intelligent."

Where is the wise man, where the scholar, where the researcher of this world? Has God not turned worldly wisdom into folly? Since, in God's wise design, the world with its wisdom did not recognize God, God chose to save believers through the folly of the cross. For Jews ask for signs, Greeks seek wisdom, while we proclaim a crucified Messiah: a scandal to Jews, folly to pagans; but to those who are called, Jews and Greeks alike, a Messiah who is the power of God and the wisdom of God. For the folly of God is wiser than men, the weakness of God stronger than men.

The Word of God

GOSPEL

Mt 28:16-20

A reading from the holy Gospel according to Saint Matthew


At that time, the eleven disciples went to Galilee, to the mountain Jesus had pointed out to them. When they saw him, they bowed down, but some hesitated.


Coming up to them, Jesus said: "Full authority has been given to me in heaven and on earth. Go and make disciples of all peoples, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit; and teaching them to keep all that I have commanded you. And know that I will be with you all days until the end of the world."

The Word of God
