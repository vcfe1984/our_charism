Monthly renewal of religious vows

(To be prayed on the 25th of each month)

O Father, who have called us to be “the unconditional ones of God”1!

O Jesus, who came “as the inflexible vanquisher of error and the gentle champion of love”!2

O Jesus, “than whom nothing better exists”3!, who have freely wrapped us “in the inextricable4 nets of grace”5 and who have chosen us to reproduce in our life your form of life: chaste, poor, obedient and Marian; therefore we now renew our four vows as a witness to the life that You lived, and your Most Holy Mother, and the Twelve Apostles, and which we wish to imitate.

O Jesus, “the good Word of the good Father”6, who “proclaim us victors at the Supper”!7

O Holy Spirit, who appeared in tongues of fire, “so that a law of fire might be preached by tongues of fire”8, make us “the salt of the earth” (Mt 5:13) and not honey!

Mary, Daughter of God the Father, Mother of God the Son and Spouse of God the Holy Spirit! obtain for us these gifts: to be unconditional ones of the Trinity, inextricably embraced by grace, faithful to the four vows, victors in the Mass, and with tongues of fire to live and preach the New Law of love established by your Son.

Amen.
