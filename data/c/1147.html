NINE-MONTH NOVENA IN PREPARATION

FOR THE CENTENNIAL

OF THE FATIMA APPARITIONS

July 31, 2016

Dear Fathers, Brothers, and Friends,

Between the 13th of May and the 13th of October in 1917 the six apparitions at Fatima —with its message of repentance, comfort, warning, and singular protection— took place. In May of next year, we will celebrate the 100th anniversary of this memorable event which has left a deep mark not only on the Church of the twentieth century, but also on Marian devotion, on the Christian conscience, and even on the actions of some Popes (pilgrimages to her shrine, the revelation of the secrets announced by the Blessed Virgin Mary to Venerable Sr. Lucia and to Blessed Jacinta and Blessed Francisco, as well as the consecration of the world to Mary’s Immaculate Heart). As is known, the prayers of our Mother, and those intentions that She asked the Church to pray for —the world and some countries in particular (Russia)— were decisive in the events of the end of the last century, in a special way in the fall of Soviet empire, one of the most anti-Christian em-pires in whole history.

Ours is a time full of lights and shadows, of hopes and of the predication of future storms, of political uncertainties and doctrinal confusion, some of them unlike ever before. Today, as in so many crucial moments, we need the particular intervention of this protective Mother and a new triumph of her Immaculate Heart. Once again the Church goes through history “amid the persecutions of the world and the consolations of God” (St. Augustine), and many hearts are troubled by anxiety and uncertainty. The proximity of the next celebration is a special opportunity to call upon our Mother with all of our strength.

A unique way to prepare for the centennial of her protecting providential help is with a novena, though not of days, but of months. Beginning in August, which starts tomorrow, we have exactly nine months before next May.

That is why, from the General House of the Institute, we want to guide and make our own initiative that was presented to us by Father Miguel Angel Fuentes.

It is the following: without overloading the regular activities with which we already express our Marian devotion, but rather by ordering and emphasizing them, we can make this work of preparation an opportunity to express a distinctive Marian devotion that should characterize us on account of our fourth vow of Marian slavery.

Our proposal consists in joining together these nine months by devoting each one of them to exalt one of the main privileges of Mary and performing some activities, of which some are listed below, as well as all those you would like to add (recitation of the Rosary in community or in family, a continuous recitation of the Rosary on some occasions, pilgrimages to her shrines, etc.).

This is an invitation for everyone: priests, men and women religious, and the lay people of our Third Order, who would like to join us in this choral prayer, ample in its dimension, just like the expansion of our Religious Family, and long-lasting, like the upcoming nine months. However, at the same time, it is a free and spontaneous response of all who wish to accept it.

The privileges that we should keep in mind in each of the months are the following (all of them can be found in the work of Antonio Royo Marin, The Virgin Mary, or in any of the good treatises on the Blessed Virgin Mary, all of which are easily found online):

1st. August: Mary’s Predestination

2nd. September: The Immaculate Conception

3rd. October: Mary’s Perpetual Virginity

4th. November: Divine Motherhood

5th. December: Spiritual Motherhood

6th. January: Marian Co-redemption

7th. February: Mary Mediatrix of All Graces

8th. March: The Assumption of Mary into Heaven

9th. April: Mary Queen of the Universe

In each of these months several activities could be performed.

1. To celebrate all Saturday Masses in honor of the Blessed Virgin Mary as the liturgy allows.

2. To preach on the Blessed Virgin Mary at least two Saturdays each month, if possible on the privilege to which the month is dedicated.

3. Offering all the rosaries that will be prayed during these months for the triumph of the Immaculate Heart of Mary over the dangers that the Church goes through in our time and asking for a special protection for persecuted Chris-tians—in a singular way those in the Middle East—and for our Religious Family and vocations.

4. Offering the Saturday hour of adoration or that of the first Saturdays in atonement for the offenses committed against the Immaculate Heart of Mary. “Have pity on the Heart of your Most Holy Mother. It is covered with thorns with which ungrateful men pierce it at every moment, and there is no one to remove them with an act of reparation” (Words of the Infant Jesus to Sr. Lucia on December 10th, 1925).

5. To fulfill monthly with the conditions that the Church requires to obtain indulgences: confession, communion, and prayers for the Pope.

6. This could be a good opportunity to renew and promote the consecration to Mary according to of St. Louis de Montfort’s method among the Third Order members and the laity in general, with the 33 days of preparation indicated by the saint.

Father Miguel Fuentes has offered to put some devotional thoughts regarding the corresponding Marian privilege into writing each month which will be sent out via email.

As it can be seen from the above, the intentions of this novena are essentially threefold:

1. To ask for the triumph of the Immaculate Heart of Mary over the dan-gers that the Church goes through in our time.

2. To request a special protection for persecuted Christians, in a singular way for those in the Middle East.

3. To intercede for our Religious Family and for vocations.

The novena should begin during the month of August this year, 2016, simply by letting everyone know that it is happening and beginning with that which can be done from day one: we must keep those intentions which are being offered as part of the novena in mind, as well as the prayers for the intentions already indicated, to which everyone can add more, if desired. The Superiors of each Province and House will then suggest other activities that can be per-formed. From what has been said above, the reading of and meditation on some classic books on Mary, such as the works of St. Louis de Montfort, St. Alphon-sus Liguori, Roschini, Antonio Royo Marin (the explanation of the aforementioned privileges can be taken from his work The Virgin Mary), and others, is also recommended.

We thank Fr. Miguel Angel Fuentes for having taken the initiative to make this nine-month novena and we wish you all a very fruitful preparation to celebrate the 100th anniversary of the apparitions of Our Lady at Cova da Iria in 2017.
