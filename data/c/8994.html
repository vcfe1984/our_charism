“A shoot shall come forth from the root of Jesse, and from his root a flower shall blossom.” (Is 11:1)

The Blessed Virgin of Luján is the Mother, Queen and principal Intercessor of our Religious Family of the Incarnate Word. For, as we have always been taught, She is the fruitful earth where the divine dew has engendered each of the vocations of our Religious Family.

And since “vocation is love that can only be repaid with love”1, today we wish to present at the feet of our Mother, the Virgin of Luján, as a perpetual testimony of our love and gratitude for the vocations that She sends to our Religious Family, this Golden Rose, which is at the same time our vote of confidence (or, as it were, payment in advance) for the many vocations that we hope to conceive through her intercession and for which we already thank her.

The Rose is 45 cm tall; it is made of silver and plated –inside and out– in 24-carat gold. All of it has been chiseled by hand, a detail that lets us glimpse God’s most personal call to souls. It is a rose with thorns, symbolizing that following Christ implies an intimate association with the Cross. And more notably, it is a rose with a bud. We wanted it so because it represents two things: the most sacred mystery of the Incarnation, which identifies us; and, on the other hand, the Mystical Rose in whom the divine dew engendered the bud of each of the vocations of our Religious Family. It has 7 leaves, above which the precious flower rises gracefully, expressing the sincere pursuit of holiness that is asked of each one of us and of the vocations we trust Our Lady will send us. For St. Bonaventure said that “whoever desires to obtain the sevenfold grace of the Holy Spirit, let him seek the flower on the stem. Through the stem let him reach the flower, and through the flower let him reach God”2. That is, let him seek Jesus in Mary, as the fundamental code of our spirituality says3, since through the stem we will reach the flower and through the flower we will find God. In this our fourth Marian vow is also made manifest, since, as our Constitutions admirably state: “Mary is ‘the proximate end, the mysterious center and the easy means of going to Christ’”4. In the petals of the rose go also our prayers asking for the perseverance, fidelity and holiness of our vocations. And finally, this rose has been made with material from Argentina and by an Argentine goldsmith, for that is the nation where She chose to stay and from which she has sent missionaries to so many places5.

Prayer

[At the moment of placing the rose at the feet of Our Lady:]

Receive, O most worthy Queen and Mother of ours, this Golden Rose that we, your children of the Religious Family of the Incarnate Word, present to you today, as a gift of perpetual gratitude for the vocations with which you bless our Institutes and as a trusting pledge of the many more that we hope to conceive through your intercession. O most pure and Immaculate Conception of Luján, may the most pure dew of your grace make fruitful in countless souls the ardent desire to consecrate themselves to your Son in this Family of the Incarnate Word, so that, going to the ends of the earth, they may give off the fragrance of your perfume, which is nothing other than peace, joy and hope.
