On the Cross in the thought of Padre Pio

Sermon delivered by Rev. Fr. Carlos Miguel Buela in the Parish of “Our Lady of Sorrows,” on September 14, 2001, on the occasion of the feast of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross

Today we celebrate the feast of the Exaltation of the Holy Cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, and on this day several of the Servants of the Lord and the Virgin of Matará are making their perpetual vows. The great St. Thomas Aquinas compares the consecration proper to religious life to nothing less than the greatest sacrifice there is, which is the holocaust, that is, the total offering of oneself as a victim to God1.

It seemed to me that for this occasion it might be profitable to put together what we may call a sort of florilegium of the Cross, following the thought of that great crucified man of the last century, Padre Pio, who, as some of you well know, has sublime thoughts on the Cross.

Pleasing God

The Cross is a pleasing of God: «To love God is to please Him, and it is not worth worrying about the rest, knowing that God will take care of us more than can be said or imagined».

Chosen

He treats each one of us, and in a special way consecrated souls, as chosen ones: «Open your heart to the heavenly physician of souls and abandon yourself with confidence into His arms. He treats you as one of His chosen and invites you to follow Him closely up the slope of Calvary».

Climbing Calvary

This is what consecrated life is about: climbing Calvary, and it is a climbing of Calvary every day, as is the life of the priest who, in going up to the altar each day, climbs a little further up Calvary. Padre Pio says: «Yes, let us take comfort when we see ourselves more and more oppressed by afflictions; let us give thanks to the divine mercy that makes us sharers in the Passion and death of our divine Master, and until it can be said of us “this Christian is another Christ,” let us not stop until we have climbed Calvary».

His love

This is the fruit of His love: «If God subjects us to a very heavy Cross, and gives us the strength needed to bear it with merit, these are unmistakable and unique signs of His love for us».

That very heavy Cross may at times be health problems, family problems; it may also be misunderstandings, temptations, or tribulations of various kinds… We must ask for those crosses. Let us think about this: «they are unmistakable and unique signs of His love for us».

Being crucified

The fact is that we must be crucified, as our divine Head is.

«Let us follow the divine Master along the slope of Calvary carrying our Cross, and when He sees fit to nail us to the Cross, let us thank Him and consider ourselves fortunate for so great an honor granted to us, knowing that to be crucified with Jesus is a far more perfect act than simply contemplating Jesus on the Cross».

Not being frightened by the Cross

That is why we must not be frightened by the Cross. There are souls that do not advance in the spiritual life out of fear of the Cross. What is more, there are souls that go backward; there are even souls that abandon Christ because they are afraid of the Cross.

«Do not let the Cross frighten you. The greatest proof of love consists in suffering for the beloved; and if God, out of so much love, suffered so much pain, the pain suffered for Him becomes lovable by reason of love»

Jesus and the Cross: bridegroom and bride indissolubly united

And things are so arranged that there is no Jesus without the Cross, nor the Cross without Jesus. Jesus the bridegroom, the Cross the bride, indissolubly united.

«Jesus is never without the Cross, but the Cross is never without Jesus».

The Cross does not crush

And because Jesus and the Cross, the Cross and Jesus, are thus united, the Cross never crushes, even though that is the impression it sometimes gives us when we have to suffer great difficulties; but the Cross does not crush. «Do not let the Cross crush you. If its weight makes you stagger, its power sustains you».

It seems that the weight makes us succumb, but at the same time it is this very weight that makes us stand firm in the combat, in the spiritual struggle that lies before us.

With the Cross on our shoulders

That is why we must climb Calvary with the Cross on our shoulders: «Let us climb Calvary with the Cross on our shoulders. Let us not hesitate. Our ascent will end with the heavenly vision of the most sweet Savior».

Life is Calvary

For life is Calvary: «Life is a calvary. It is fitting to climb it joyfully».

The Cross is a banner

And the Cross is a banner: «The Cross is the banner of the chosen. Let us not separate ourselves from it, and we shall sing victory in every battle».

The harder, the more merit

And the harder, the heavier the Cross, the more merit we will have: «The harder the trial God sends His chosen ones, the more abundantly He comforts them during the oppression and exalts them after the struggle»,

Love and suffering

It is the mystery that always goes together: love and suffering, suffering and love. The more suffering, the more love. The more love, the more suffering: «Whoever begins to love must be prepared to suffer».

Suffering and happiness

«The life of the Christian is nothing but a continual struggle against oneself. Happiness is not attained except by means of suffering».

That is why on this day, dear brothers and sisters, let us ask the Lord, through His Mother, the Blessed Virgin Mary, that we may come to have within us the science of the Cross; for it is a grace of God to have the wisdom of the Cross. And when we have the wisdom of the Cross we will have that other great thing which only the Cross gives: the joy of the Cross.

We ask this of Our Lord, who was the first to suffer it for our sake, and of His Mother, who was the second, suffering spiritually all the sorrows that her Son suffered out of love for us.

May we always be friends of the Cross, may we never flee from it, because whoever flees from the Cross flees from Jesus, and whoever flees from Jesus will never find happiness.

Words before the blessing

What a contrast between the terrifying images of the catastrophe of the Twin Towers, which we have seen in these last days and which has shaken every man and woman of good will in every part of the world, and a beautiful ceremony like this one, where, in peace and with great joy, young women fully aware of what they are doing consecrate themselves for their whole life to the Lord of all creation. I congratulate these sisters, and I congratulate in a special way all their relatives, friends and acquaintances, and also all those who have prayed to make it possible for these young women to consecrate themselves totally to God. I also congratulate their superiors, and the fathers and priests who have accompanied them spiritually.

I hope that this may truly be a sign of a better world for all, a more human world, a less cruel world, a world where the love of God truly shines for all men.
