Mass of St. John of the Cross,

Priest and Doctor of the Church

Entrance Antiphon Gal 6:14

God forbid that I should glory in anything save in the Cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, through which the world is crucified to me, and I to the world.

Collect

God, our Father,
who made your priest St. John of the Cross
a perfect model of self-denial
and of love for the Cross,
help us to imitate his life on earth
so as to come to enjoy your glory in heaven.
Through our Lord Jesus Christ.

Liturgy of the Word

First Reading (1 Cor 2:1-10)

A reading from the first letter of the apostle St. Paul to the Corinthians

Brothers: When I came to your city to proclaim the Gospel to you, I did not seek to do so by means of eloquent speech or human wisdom, but resolved to speak to you of nothing but Jesus Christ, and more than that, of Jesus Christ crucified.

I came before you weak and trembling with fear. When I spoke to you and preached the Gospel to you, I did not wish to persuade you with the words of a wise man; on the contrary, I persuaded you by means of the Spirit and the power of God, so that your faith might rest on the power of God and not on the wisdom of men.

It is true that to those who are mature in the faith we preach wisdom, but not the wisdom of this world nor that of those who rule the world, who are going to be brought to nothing. On the contrary, we preach a divine, mysterious wisdom, which has remained hidden and which was foreseen by God before the ages, to lead us to glory. None of those who rule this world knew it, for, had they known it, they would never have crucified the Lord of glory.

But what we preach is, as Scripture says, that what God has prepared for those who love him, eye has not seen, nor ear heard, nor has the mind of man even been able to imagine it. To us, however, God has revealed it through the Spirit, who knows all things perfectly, even the depths of God.

Gospel Lk 14:25-33

A reading from the holy Gospel according to St. Luke

At that time, a great crowd was walking with Jesus, and he, turning to his disciples, said to them: “If anyone wishes to follow me and does not prefer me to his father and his mother, to his wife and his children, to his brothers and his sisters, and even to himself, he cannot be my disciple. And whoever does not carry his Cross and follow me cannot be my disciple.

For which of you, wishing to build a tower, does not first sit down to calculate the cost, to see whether he has enough to finish it? Otherwise, after laying the foundations, he may be unable to complete it, and all who hear of it will begin to mock him, saying: ‘This man began to build and could not finish.’

Or what king, going to fight against another king, does not first sit down to consider whether he will be able with ten thousand soldiers to go out to meet the one who comes against him with twenty thousand? For if not, while the other is still far away, he will send an embassy to propose terms of peace.

So, then, any one of you who does not renounce all his possessions cannot be my disciple.”

The Gospel of the Lord

Prayer of the Faithful

Let us now turn to God the Father, who wishes to reveal his glory to us, and let us present our prayer to him, asking that his salvation may soon reach all men.

1.- That God may visit, renew and strengthen the Church with the gifts of his grace. Let us pray to the Lord.

2.- That families may accept with courage and joy the call of their members to the priesthood. Let us pray to the Lord.

3.- That the peace of God may extinguish all hatred and mistrust, and that all may live reconciled with one another. Let us pray to the Lord.

4.- That the light of Christ may dispel the darkness of those who are sick and who suffer for any reason. Let us pray to the Lord.

5.- That, following the example of St. John of the Cross, we may seek the face of God and give ourselves to prayer and to the contemplation of the mysteries of his love. Let us pray to the Lord.

Almighty God, may the splendor of your glory dawn in our hearts, so that, the darkness of night being dispelled, the coming of your Only-Begotten may show that we are children of the light. Through our Lord Jesus Christ.

Prayer over the Offerings

Accept, O Lord, these gifts which we present to you
on the feast of St. John of the Cross,
and grant that all of us who celebrate
the mysteries of the Lord's Passion
may faithfully show forth in our lives
what we celebrate in the Eucharist.
Through Jesus Christ our Lord.

Preface

V. The Lord be with you.
R. And with your spirit.

V. Lift up your hearts.
R. We lift them up to the Lord.

V. Let us give thanks to the Lord our God.
R. It is right and just.

It is truly right and just,
our duty and our salvation, to give you thanks
always and everywhere,
Lord, Holy Father,
almighty and eternal God.

And to proclaim your praises
on the solemnity of St. John of the Cross, our Father,
through Jesus Christ, your beloved Son.

Through Him, who is your Word, you made all things,
leaving them clothed in his goodness and beauty.
He has given us, Father,
the fullness of revelation and of grace,
for in your one Word
you told us everything and gave us everything
in heaven and on earth.

By the paschal mystery of his glorious passion
he reconciled humanity with you,
gave it fullness of life through his Spirit,
and raised the whole of creation up to heaven.

And now he lives in glory in heaven
as Bridegroom and Head of the Church,
surrounded by all the saints
who form his eternal crown.

Therefore, united with the angelic choirs,
we acclaim you, full of joy:

Holy, Holy, Holy…

Communion Antiphon Mt 16:24

Whoever wishes to come with me, let him deny himself, take up his Cross and follow me, says the Lord.

Prayer after Communion

Lord our God,
who wonderfully illuminated
the mystery of the Passion
in the life of St. John of the Cross;
grant us now,
strengthened by this sacrifice,
to remain always united to Christ by faith
and to labor in the Church
for the Salvation of all men.
Through Jesus Christ our Lord.
